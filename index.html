Know about IT Infra and Cyber Security

What is IT infrastructure?

The technology a company runs on every day: servers, networks, storage, cloud services, laptops and the software that connects them.

What is cyber security?

The practice of protecting that technology and the data in it from theft, misuse and attacks.

The three goals

  • Confidentiality: only the right people see the data.
  • Integrity: data stays accurate and unaltered.
  • Availability: systems work when people need them.

Why they work together

Infrastructure keeps the business running. Security keeps it safe. Each team on the right plays a part in both, and all report up to the CISO.
